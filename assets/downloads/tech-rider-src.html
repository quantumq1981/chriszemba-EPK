Performance format: Scalable 4-6 piece professional live band. Final personnel and instrumentation are confirmed for each engagement. Typical configurations may include lead vocal/guitar, bass, drums, keyboards, additional vocalist, saxophone, or another featured instrumentalist.

Stage & Performance Configuration

4-Piece

  • Recommended stage: approximately 16' W x 10' D
  • Typical core: guitar/lead vocal, bass, drums, keys or second featured musician
  • Approximately 4 independent monitor mixes

5-6 Piece

  • Recommended stage: approximately 20' W x 12' D
  • Instrumentation expands according to contracted lineup
  • Smaller footprints may be accommodated by advance agreement

Typical layout: drums upstage center on a 6' x 8' rug; keys upstage left when applicable; guitar/lead vocal and bass/vocal downstage. Provide clean, grounded 120V AC power at musician positions.

Audio / AV Requirements

  • Professional FOH PA appropriate to room and attendance
  • Qualified FOH audio engineer
  • Console with adequate inputs and routing
  • Stage monitoring / wedges or equivalent
  • Vocal microphones with boom stands
  • Instrument microphones and DI boxes as required
  • Required XLR cabling and microphone stands
  • Safe AC power distribution across the stage

Zemba Music Co. is accustomed to working with casino, resort, corporate, and third-party production teams. Mic, DI, and monitoring preferences are flexible, and we are happy to coordinate directly with the production provider.

Monitoring, Soundcheck & Volume

Monitoring

  • Approximately 4 independent monitor mixes for a standard 4-6 piece configuration
  • Typical mixes: Guitar/Vocal, Drums, Keys/Vocal, Bass/Vocal
  • Final monitor needs depend on contracted personnel

Soundcheck

  • 45-60 minutes preferred after production is fully operational
  • Stage and audio system should be ready before artist soundcheck
  • Final schedule coordinated with production in advance
Corporate / networking receptions: Band and production will work together to maintain an energetic musical presentation at an appropriate SPL for the event. Volume can be managed to allow comfortable guest conversation without sacrificing musical impact.

Backline & Self-Contained Production

Musicians provide personal instruments and standard personal performance equipment. Any venue-supplied backline is coordinated in advance.

Optional production: Self-contained PA and basic lighting may be available by advance arrangement for select engagements. It is not included unless specifically stated in the contract or quote.

Hospitality

  • Bottled water for all performers
  • Non-alcoholic beverages
  • Light meal or substantial snacks when call time overlaps a normal meal period
  • Secure green room, dressing room, or performer holding area when available
  • Reasonable restroom access
  • No alcoholic beverages are required

Load-In, Parking & Access

  • Artist / load-in entrance and loading zone
  • Parking validation or complimentary artist parking where applicable
  • Venue or security credentials
  • Stage access and soundcheck times
  • Venue security procedures

Production Coordination

Final personnel, stage plot, input list, run-of-show, set times, and any event-specific production requirements will be confirmed in advance. Zemba Music Co. is happy to coordinate directly with the client's AV or production company to ensure an efficient load-in, soundcheck, and performance.
