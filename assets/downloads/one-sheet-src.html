About

Chris Zemba is a Las Vegas–based guitarist, vocalist, and bandleader with more than 25 years on stage, delivering high-energy Blues Rock, R&B, and Soul alongside contemporary Top 40, rock, pop, and country. He has performed at Caesars Entertainment, Station Casinos, and Boyd Gaming properties, and Mohegan Sun’s Wolf Den, and currently plays lead guitar and directs the seven-piece horn band DSJ. From solo acoustic to full band, every set is tailored to the room.
