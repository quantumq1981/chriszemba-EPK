Two ways to book the acoustic set

Solo or Duo

Same repertoire, same bandleader — scaled to your room, your budget, and your run-of-show. Not sure which fits? Send the date and you'll get a recommendation.

Most flexible footprint

Solo Acoustic

Chris on guitar and lead vocals — the smallest, quietest, most adaptable way to put live music in the room. Ideal when you want atmosphere and connection without giving up floor space or the budget for a full band.

Best for

Wedding ceremonies Cocktail hours Restaurant & bar sets Lounge residencies Dinner & background
  • Vocal + acoustic guitar via DI
  • Single 120V drop · compact footprint
  • Self-contained PA available, or plug into the house
Fuller sound

Acoustic Duo

Add a second player on keys or percussion for a richer, more dynamic sound that still fits a tight stage. The duo carries a bigger room, lifts cocktail energy into a reception, and gives you real dance-floor moments.

Best for

Receptions Cocktail → party Mid-size lounges Corporate mixers Private parties
  • Guitar + keys or percussion
  • 2 monitor mixes · scales to room and volume
  • Self-contained PA available, or plug into the house

Simple and low-risk

How booking works

Three steps from first email to downbeat. No surprises, and no chasing paperwork.

1

Inquiry

Send your date, room, and vibe through the booking form — spec solo or duo, or just send the date. You get a professional reply within one business day, usually with a quote and a recommendation.

2

Hold & contract

We place a courtesy hold on your date and send a simple contract. W‑9 and certificate of insurance come up front, so your venue's paperwork is handled before you ask.

3

Advance & downbeat

About a week out, we lock the final details: set times, song requests, and run-of-show. On the day, we arrive early, set up quietly, and deliver exactly the show you booked.
