The bandleader

About Chris Zemba

Chris Zemba is a dynamic Las Vegas–based guitarist, vocalist, and bandleader known for high-energy performances spanning Blues Rock, R&B, and Soul. Blending contemporary Top 40, rock, pop, and country, he tailors every show to connect directly with his audience.

With more than 25 years of professional experience, Chris has performed at premier venues across Southern Nevada—including Caesars Entertainment, Station Casinos, and Boyd Gaming properties—as well as Mohegan Sun's iconic Wolf Den. As lead guitarist and musical director for The Rowdy Band (Rowdy McCaren), he played a central role in their 2012 KWNR Vegas Country Battle of the Bands victory and their showcase at the National Finals Rodeo at the Thomas & Mack Center. Recently, Chris joined DSJ (formerly Down South Jukers) as lead guitarist and musical director, where his seasoned stage presence helped elevate the 7-piece horn band into one of Las Vegas's premier acts, delivering a signature, horn-driven mix of classic rock, soul, and R&B.

Since 2011, Chris has owned and operated Zemba Entertainment, managing multiple acts and partnering with top talent agencies to craft unforgettable entertainment for casino, corporate, and private events.

House-friendly

Every input preference is flexible. We work with your house inventory, console, and run-of-show.

Full Band, 4 to 5 piece

  • Drums upstage center on 6' x 8' rug, keys upstage left
  • Guitar/lead vocal and bass/vocal downstage
  • 3 vocal positions on boom stands
  • 4 monitor mixes (Gtr/Voc, Drums, Keys/Voc, Bass/Voc)
  • 120V power drop at each position

Solo

  • Vocal + acoustic guitar via DI
  • 1 monitor mix
  • Single 120V drop
  • Self-contained PA available

Duo / Trio

  • Guitar + keys, or guitar + bass + drums
  • Input package scaled from full band
  • 2 to 3 monitor mixes
  • Scales to room and volume
